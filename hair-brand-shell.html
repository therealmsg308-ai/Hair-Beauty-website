WHAT MAKES K-TIPS DIFFERENT

K-Tip extensions — also known as keratin bond or fusion extensions — are the most seamless, most natural-looking extension method available. Each individual strand is pre-tipped with Italian keratin protein and fused directly to a small section of your natural hair using a professional fusion heat tool. The result is hair that moves, behaves, and feels completely like your own — because the bond is virtually invisible and sits flat against the scalp.

At Hair Deity, Eva Marie takes this a step further. Every K-Tip install is custom designed to the exact density of YOUR hair — the strand count, placement pattern, and bond size are all mapped specifically to your head before a single strand is fused. No two installs are the same. This is why a virtual consultation and photos are required before your appointment is confirmed.

WHAT ARE BRAZILIAN KNOTS

Brazilian Knots are an individual strand-by-strand extension method that uses fine elastic thread to attach extension hair to your natural hair — no glue, no heat tools, no metal beads. The result is a completely free-flowing, lightweight install that moves exactly like natural hair and is virtually undetectable at the scalp.

This technique is especially beloved for natural 4A–4C hair because it works with your natural curl pattern rather than against it. When moisture hits, your natural hair and the extension curl together — creating a seamless, uniform texture throughout. Eva Marie has mastered this technique to create installs so natural, even other stylists cannot detect them.

WHY GODDESSES CHOOSE BRAZILIAN KNOTS

🧵

NO GLUE · NO HEAT · NO METAL

Only fine elastic thread is used — making this one of the gentlest extension methods available. Zero chemicals, zero heat damage, zero heavy beads weighing down your hair.

🌿

MOST GENTLE ON NATURAL HAIR

Brazilian Knots protect and preserve your natural hair during wear. Many clients report their natural hair growing significantly during a Brazilian Knots install because the scalp remains clean and accessible.

💧

WASH AND GO FRIENDLY

You can wash your hair, work out, and swim — the knots hold securely for 3 to 4 months. For Kinky Curly installs, moisture actually enhances the look as natural and extension curl together.

🎨

COMPLETELY FREE FLOWING

Unlike sew-ins, all of your natural hair is out and accessible. You can part your hair anywhere, wear it up or down, and style it in any direction.

⚡

NO CONSULTATION REQUIRED

Unlike K-Tips, Brazilian Knots do not require a virtual consultation first. Book directly, confirm your texture with The Oracle, and Eva Marie handles the rest.

WHAT IS A TRADITIONAL SEW-IN

A Traditional Sew-In is the most time-tested, most protective extension method available. Your natural hair is first braided flat to the scalp in cornrows — creating a clean, flat foundation. Then Eva Marie sews weft hair extensions directly onto those cornrows using a curved C-needle and premium black thread — row by row, stitch by stitch, from the nape of your neck to your crown.

The result is full, voluminous, completely natural-looking hair that protects your natural hair underneath for 6 to 8 weeks. No glue. No heat on the bonds. Just needle, thread, and Eva Marie's skilled hands.
